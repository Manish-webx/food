From Real Fruit to Your Glass: How Nere Makes Beverages

June 21, 2026 4 min read

A good beverage should do more than look appealing. It should taste refreshing, feel thoughtfully made and let its ingredients speak for themselves. At Nere, we create real fruit beverages that bring together familiar flavours, quality ingredients and simple, enjoyable hydration.

From selecting the fruit to balancing the final recipe, every drink goes through a process of testing, tasting and refinement before it reaches your glass.

Here is how Nere beverages come together.

It Begins With a Simple Question

Every Nere drink starts with one question: would we genuinely look forward to drinking this?

We do not want to create beverages only because a flavour is trending or photographs well. The drink must have a clear purpose in the menu and suit a real moment in someone’s day—whether that is a refreshing lemonade at an event, a cold-pressed juice in the morning or a smoothie when something more filling is needed.

This approach helps us develop real fruit beverages that are enjoyable, approachable and easy to understand.

Choosing Fruit for Flavour

Fruit is naturally different from batch to batch. Its sweetness, acidity, colour and aroma can vary depending on the variety, season and stage of ripeness. That is part of working with real ingredients.

We select fruit by looking at more than appearance. A bright orange may not always be the juiciest, and a perfectly shaped mango may not have the strongest flavour. What matters most is how the fruit contributes to the finished beverage.

Seasonality also influences our choices. Using fruits when they are naturally flavourful helps us create real fruit beverages with a fresher and more distinctive character.

Finding the Right Balance

More fruit does not automatically guarantee a better drink. A beverage needs balance.

Too much citrus can make it overly sharp. Excessive sweetness can cover the natural fruit flavour. Too much water can leave the drink tasting flat. Herbs, spices and garnishes can enhance a recipe, but only when they support the main ingredients rather than compete with them.

During recipe development, we adjust:

  • Fruit and liquid proportions
  • Sweetness and acidity
  • Texture and consistency
  • Herbs, spices and infusions
  • Serving temperature
  • Garnish and presentation

Every adjustment is made with the complete drinking experience in mind. This testing process allows our real fruit beverages to remain flavourful, refreshing and easy to enjoy.

Small Batches Allow Greater Attention

Nere beverages are currently developed and prepared in small batches. This gives us greater control over flavour, consistency and presentation.

Instead of treating a recipe as fixed after the first successful trial, we continue observing how it performs in real situations. We note how the flavour changes when chilled, how the ingredients hold together and how customers respond after tasting it.

Small-batch preparation helps us pay closer attention to the details that shape real fruit beverages, especially while recipes are being tested and improved.

What Goes Into a Nere Drink?

Different drinks require different ingredients and preparation methods.

Our cold-pressed juices are designed to let the natural fruit flavour remain central. Our lemonades and coolers combine citrus and fruit-led flavours with complementary ingredients such as mint, ginger or carefully selected infusions. Our smoothies may include fruit, milk or plant-based alternatives, cocoa, oats and other ingredients that add texture and substance.

The objective is not to make every beverage identical. It is to ensure that each recipe has a clear flavour identity.

This gives Nere’s real fruit beverages variety while keeping the overall menu connected by a simple idea: drinks should taste as good as they look.

Why We Test Before We Finalise

Product development rarely happens perfectly on the first attempt.

A recipe may sound exciting but taste unbalanced. A drink that works in a kitchen trial may behave differently when prepared for several people. A fruit garnish may look beautiful but affect the taste if handled incorrectly. Even the shape of a cup or bottle can influence how a beverage is served and experienced.

Testing helps us answer practical questions:

  • Is the fruit flavour noticeable?
  • Is the drink too sweet or too sharp?
  • Does it remain enjoyable when properly chilled?
  • Is the portion satisfying?
  • Can the recipe be prepared consistently?
  • Does the packaging suit the drink?
  • Would someone want to order it again?

Feedback from every trial helps us improve our real fruit beverages before introducing them more widely.

Prepared for the Way You Will Drink It

The final preparation depends on the beverage.

Cold-pressed juices are bottled and chilled. Lemonades, coolers and mocktails are assembled to preserve their freshness and visual layers. Smoothies and shakes are blended to create the intended texture and served in packaging suited to the drink.

Temperature also matters. A beverage that is meant to be refreshing should reach you properly chilled, wihout relying on excessive ice that dilutes its flavour.

By considering the complete journey -from preparation to serving- we make sure our real fruit beverages deliver the experience intended by the recipe.

Real Ingredients, Without Complicated Language

Beverage labels and menus are often filled with confusing descriptions. At Nere, we prefer to tell people what they are drinking in plain language.

If a beverage contains orange, lemon, ginger, mint or another key ingredient, that should be easy to recognise. We want customers to choose their drinks based on flavour, ingredients and the occasion, not because of exaggerated promises.

Our focus is on creating real fruit beverages that feel honest, joyful and thoughtfully prepared.

From Our Trials to Your Glass

Nere is still growing, testing and listening. Every market trial, event and customer conversation teaches us something about what people want from their beverages.

Some people prefer something light and citrusy. Others want a bold fruit flavour, a refreshing fizz or a more filling smoothie. These differences help us build a menu with something for different tastes and moments.

From choosing ingredients and refining proportions to chilling, packaging and serving, every step has one purpose: to create real fruit beverages that people genuinely enjoy drinking.

That is how Nere takes fruit from its natural starting point and turns it into something refreshing for your glass.
