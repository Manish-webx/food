Our Story

Hydration shouldn't be an afterthought.

Somewhere along the way, drinks became louder than the moments they were meant for. More sugar. More flavours. More ingredients we couldn't pronounce.

We wanted to go in the opposite direction.

Nere was created with a simple belief: good hydration doesn't need to be complicated.

We believe everyday beverages should begin with real ingredients, balanced sweetness, and flavours people already love, like they've always belonged on our tables.

Every recipe begins with one question:
Would we happily drink this ourselves every morning?

If the answer is no, it doesn't make the menu.

We're still small.
We're still learning.
And we're building Nere one customer, one recipe, and one morning at a time.

Join us for a sip at Nere.
